Welcome to Cardi-GPT

Cardi-GPT is an AI chatbot that interprets ECG reports and explains heart signals using intelligent medical analysis through chatbot.

Models

Deep Learning models trained on annotated ECG datasets to detect rhythm abnormalities other cardiac conditions.

Diagnosis

The process of mapping ECG waveforms and intervals to potential clinical conditions.

Chatbot

Guides users through ECG interpretation by asking structured questions, explaining waveform features.

Category

ECG interpretations are typically categorized into normal, abnormal but non-critical and critical abnormalities.

How It Works

1. Upload ECG

Users upload or record their ECG data through a secure interface.

2. AI Analysis

Our deep learning model processes and interprets ECG waveforms.

3. Chatbot Assistance

The chatbot explains findings and answers user questions in simple terms.

4. Report & Insights

Users receive an easy-to-read ECG report with recommendations.
