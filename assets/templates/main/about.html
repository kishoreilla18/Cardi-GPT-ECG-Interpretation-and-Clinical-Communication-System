About Us

CardiGPT is an AI-powered health assistant designed to provide accurate and user-friendly information about cardiology and general healthcare. It helps users understand medical terms, diagnostic inputs, and treatment options in a simple way, while also offering guidance on lifestyle and preventive care for better heart health. CardiGPT aims to make healthcare knowledge more accessible, empowering individuals to take informed steps toward their well-being.
